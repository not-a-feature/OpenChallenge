# Participation rules

This example is open for local testing and has no closing date. Daily quotas reset at midnight UTC.

- Register with an available sign-in provider, or use the local demonstration account when available.
- Each account may belong to one team per challenge.
- A team can contain up to 10 participants. Members share an invitation code.
- Each team may submit up to 10 uploads per day. Files rejected by worker validation also count toward this quota.
- Any team member can submit predictions. Only the team owner can manage the team.
- Submitted files are private to the team and organizers; aggregate published metrics are public.
- Upload predictions only. Executable participant code is never run.

## Leaderboard phases

During the public phase, each team's best completed submission per track appears in the leaderboard. Ties share rank. Once frozen, the public leaderboard stops accepting newly completed results. The final phase selects the latest accepted upload per team and track before the freeze cutoff, with metrics shown only after publication. An invalid final upload is visible as an invalid selection rather than silently replaced by an older submission.

The organizer controls freezing and final publication. Rules for a real competition should be reviewed and published before registration opens.
