DNA foundation models encode sequences as dense representations. This benchmark example asks participants to reconstruct the original sequence and quantify what those representations reveal.

The associated [study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06950) investigates **DNABERT-2, Evo 2, and Nucleotide Transformer v2**, comparing per-token and pooled representations.

This scaffold uses **small synthetic sequence fixtures** to demonstrate uploads and evaluation. Model-labelled tracks share one fixture; no model embeddings or published benchmark results are provided.
