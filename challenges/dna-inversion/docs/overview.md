# DNA Inversion Challenge

How much of a DNA sequence can be recovered from its embedding? This example is inspired by **How Private Are DNA Embeddings? Inverting Foundation Model Representations of Genomic Sequences**, by Sofiane Ouaari, Jules Kreuer, and Nico Pfeifer.

The paper examines reconstruction from DNABERT-2, Evo 2, and Nucleotide Transformer v2 representations, including per-token and mean-pooled embeddings.

This portal is a **synthetic demonstration**, not an official competition or reproduction of the paper's results. Its three tracks share a small synthetic reference set. Model names organize the example workflow; there are no pretrained model embeddings in the download.

## Participate

1. Sign in and create or join a team.
2. Download the synthetic examples from the data page.
3. Upload a ZIP or compressed TAR archive containing one CSV.
4. Follow validation and scoring in your submission history.
5. Compare public results on the leaderboard.
